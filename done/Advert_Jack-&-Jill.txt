Job Description

As a Data Scientist at cloro, you will transform a unique corpus of AI search data into public-facing research and internal monitoring tools. You'll mine crawler behavior and citations to track how engines like Perplexity and Gemini evolve. This role is perfect for a quantitative researcher who loves writing and messy web data.

Location

    Join a high-velocity team that hit $1M ARR in under three months, offering the stability of traction with the agility of a startup.

What You Will Do

    Mine the monitoring corpus for findings worth publishing, including citations, share of voice, SERP features, and crawler behavior.
    Build automated change tracking and drift detection systems to identify when AI search engines alter their behavior or citation patterns.
    Author and publish data-backed /research/ studies and posts that translate complex statistical findings into clear, compelling narratives for the industry.

The ideal candidate

    3-6 years of experience in data science, analytics, or quantitative research with a portfolio of clear, insightful technical writing.
    Advanced proficiency in SQL and Python to navigate and extract insights from large, messy datasets like scraped SERPs and LLM responses.
    Exceptional statistical judgment to distinguish between genuine search engine behavior shifts and artifacts of data collection methodologies.
