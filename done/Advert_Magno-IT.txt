What You'll Be Doing

    Develop Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines to improve search quality and information accuracy. 
    Create recommendation, ranking, and semantic search capabilities using modern AI frameworks. 
    Integrate structured and unstructured knowledge sources, including metadata, taxonomies, and ontologies. 
    Define evaluation strategies to measure model quality, reliability, and business impact.
