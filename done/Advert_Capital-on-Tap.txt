What You'll Be Doing

We're hiring a Senior Data Scientist to join Capital on Tap, reporting to the Head of FP&A.

You'll own two linked workstreams - IFRS9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL), and portfolio modelling that feeds the financial model to produce forecasts. The work spans the UK and US revolving credit books, sitting close to Finance, Risk and Data Science.

Tech stack: Snowflake, dbt, Fivetran, Python, JupyterLab, Omni, and Kubernetes on Azure.

ECL

    Build, calibrate, implement and monitor IFRS9 models (PD, LGD, EAD), including staging/SICR logic and macroeconomic overlays.
    Run and harden month-end ECL production - scenario runs, combined outputs, and result checks into Snowflake.

Portfolio Modelling

    Develop and maintain the portfolio model that feeds the financial model - active customers, balances, funding, repayments, finance charges and bad debt.
    Diagnose drift, recalibrate assumptions and overlays, and deliver forecast inputs Finance can trust.

Across Both

    Partner with FP&A, Risk and other stakeholders so model outputs land cleanly in the financial forecast, without unnecessary process or IT complexity.
    Use AI tools confidently across the workflow, and help the team adopt practical AI-assisted ways of working.

We're Looking For

    2-5+ years building statistical/credit risk models in Python, and extracting and shaping data in SQL.
    Credit risk modelling experience, ideally IFRS9 ECL (PD/LGD/EAD), staging and model monitoring - or a clear desire to build that expertise quickly.
    Commercial understanding of revolving credit economics (utilisation, arrears, default, recoveries, portfolio growth).
    Comfort working end-to-end - from analysis and calibration through to production runs and stakeholder-ready forecast inputs.
    Curiosity and judgement in applying AI to modelling and engineering work, while keeping outputs explainable and controlled.
    Ability to work autonomously with strong judgement and proactivity in a small team.

Nice to Have

    Experience with some of our stack is helpful - Snowflake, dbt, Fivetran, Python, JupyterLab, Omni, Kubernetes on Azure.
    Experience feeding portfolio/behavioural models into an FP&A or financial planning model.
    Familiarity with LightGBM or similar gradient-boosted models for behavioural forecasts.
    Exposure to model change control, independent validation, or year-end ECL disclosure packs.
    Prior work across both UK and US (or multi-region) credit portfolios.
