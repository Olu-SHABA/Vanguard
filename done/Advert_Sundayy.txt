About The Role

We are looking for a highly experienced Principal Data Scientist specializing in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence to join our team on a full-time basis. This leadership role involves designing, developing, and deploying advanced AI and machine learning solutions tailored for the regulated financial sector. The successful candidate will spearhead innovative projects focused on agentic workflows and generative AI systems, ensuring all solutions adhere to strict financial regulations and governance standards. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams including engineering, product management, and compliance to translate complex business needs into scalable technical solutions. Additionally, you will mentor junior data scientists and engineers, fostering a culture of continuous learning and technical excellence.

Qualifications

    Strong expertise in Machine Learning, with a proven track record of developing and deploying models
    Hands-on experience with agentic systems and autonomous workflows
    Proven experience with Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs)
    Experience working within regulated financial environments such as banking or fintech
    Proficiency in Python programming language
    Knowledge of financial regulations, model governance, and compliance standards
    Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills
    Strong communication and collaboration abilities

Responsibilities

    Lead the development, training, and deployment of machine learning models for financial applications
    Design and implement agentic workflows to enable autonomous decision-making systems
    Architect and scale Generative AI solutions, including systems based on large language models
    Work closely with stakeholders to understand business requirements and translate them into technical solutions
    Ensure all models and AI systems comply with relevant financial regulations and internal governance standards
    Mentor and guide junior data scientists and engineers, fostering a collaborative team environment
    Collaborate with cross-functional teams including engineering, product, and compliance to ensure seamless integration of AI solutions
    Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI, ML, and regulatory requirements to continuously enhance solutions
