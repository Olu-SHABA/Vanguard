About the role


We are looking for a Senior Data Scientist to join SMG. In this role, you will lead the technical development of our measurement and advanced analytics capability, creating robust methodologies and models that improve how retail media is measured across our Retail Media Networks.


You’ll be a hands-on technical authority, designing, coding, testing and validating analytical solutions, while developing a small team and partnering with Data Engineering, Platform and Product to turn great methodologies into scalable, production-ready solutions.


What you’ll do


Your days will be dynamic. You might find yourself:


    Leading the design and development of advanced retail media measurement methodologies using customer, transaction, campaign and exposure data.
    Developing our capabilities across areas including attribution, incrementality and causal inference, deduplicated reach and frequency, customer lifetime value, optimisation and dynamic impression modelling.
    Identifying gaps in our measurement capability and exploring emerging statistical and machine-learning techniques, taking promising ideas from feasibility through to validated prototypes.
    Partnering with Data Engineering, Platform and Product teams to turn proven methodologies into scalable, production-ready solutions.
    Making existing analytical solutions more efficient and reliable through reusable code, robust QA, clear documentation and reduced manual intervention.
    Acting as a technical authority on advanced measurement, advising teams on complex analytical challenges and ensuring approaches are rigorous and commercially useful.
    Leading and developing a small team of data analysts and data scientists, setting priorities, reviewing methodology and code, and supporting their professional growth.


What we’re looking for


    Experience in marketing effectiveness or advanced analytics, with expertise across areas such as attribution, experimental design, causal inference, regression, forecasting, segmentation and machine learning.
    Advanced Python and SQL skills, with experience building modular, well-tested and maintainable analytical code.
    Practical experience with cloud data platforms such as Snowflake, Databricks and/or Abacus.
    Excellent communication skills, with the ability to make complex methodologies and findings clear and meaningful to non-technical audiences and senior stakeholders.
    Ideally, experience leading or mentoring analysts or data scientists and reviewing methodology, code and output quality.
    Ideally, experience working with large-scale customer, transaction, campaign and first-party datasets, particularly within retail media or marketing measurement.
    Familiarity with digital advertising and measurement platforms, such as Google Analytics, Criteo, ad servers or retail media platforms, would be a plus.
