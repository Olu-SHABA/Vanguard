Lead Data Scientist

We are looking for a Lead Data Scientist to lead the design and delivery of machine learning, statistical modelling and experimentation solutions that solve complex business problems and drive measurable outcomes across our client base. You will combine strong hands-on technical expertise with consulting, delivery leadership and stakeholder management skills, guiding project teams and translating data into clear, actionable insight.

The role focuses on leading end-to-end data science delivery, developing scalable data science solutions, validating performance through robust experimentation, supporting technical best practice and partnering with client stakeholders to ensure analytical outputs deliver clear commercial impact.

About the Role

As a Lead Data Scientist, you will be a hands-on technical leader responsible for leading the development of machine learning and statistical solutions that deliver meaningful value for our clients. You will work closely with stakeholders to understand complex business challenges, shape analytical approaches, guide technical delivery and turn sophisticated outputs into clear, actionable recommendations.

This is a great opportunity for an experienced data scientist to take ownership of high-impact project delivery across multiple commercial sectors, lead small teams and play a visible role in strengthening our Advanced Data capability.
What You Will Be Doing

    Leading the development and implementation of machine learning and statistical models to solve practical, high-value business problems
    Partnering with client stakeholders to understand objectives, shape analytical approaches, influence decisions and communicate findings with confidence and clarity
    Translating complex technical outputs into clear, transparent and commercially relevant recommendations that support effective decision-making
    Identifying opportunities to create customer, operational and commercial value through advanced analytics, experimentation and data-driven innovation
    Leading small multi-disciplinary teams across data science, engineering, strategy and design to deliver high-quality, scalable outcomes
    Applying emerging data science techniques, including practical applications of Large Language Models, and helping translate them into robust, client-ready solutions
    Mentoring and coaching data scientists, reviewing analytical work and helping embed best practice across the Advanced Data team

What We Would Like You to Have

    Strong academic background in mathematics, statistics, science, engineering or another numerate discipline
    Minimum 2:1 degree in a STEM subject, with a postgraduate qualification in a quantitative field considered beneficial
    Strong knowledge of statistics, including experimental design, uncertainty quantification, causal inference and predictive modelling
    Significant experience applying supervised and unsupervised machine learning techniques in commercial or client-facing environments
    Advanced proficiency in Python and/or R, with experience operationalising data science solutions and working with modern data science tooling
    Excellent communication and consulting skills, with the ability to explain complex analysis clearly and credibly to technical and non-technical audiences
    Strong problem-solving skills, commercial judgement and the ability to lead project delivery while supporting high-performing teams
    Strong understanding of commercial value drivers such as revenue, cost, profitability, customer behaviour and operational performance, and how data science can influence them
    Experience mentoring data scientists, reviewing analytical approaches and contributing to technical standards and reusable assets
