Data Scientist (Various Levels Exec/Senior/Mid - Minimum 3 years experience)

Multiple Regions, Global Hubs – Fully Remote

Competitive, dependent on location


About the role

We're hiring Data Scientists to turn client data into insight and product decisions. You'll work directly with delivery teams on client sites.


Responsibilities

Analyse client data to surface insights that shape recommendations. Build models and statistical approaches suited to each brief. Communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical audiences. Partner with engineers to move analysis into production where needed. Keep methods sound and defensible under client scrutiny.


What we are looking for

Strong background in statistics, machine learning or a related field. Practical experience with Python, SQL and standard data tooling. Able to translate ambiguous business questions into a clear analytical approach. Comfortable presenting findings to clients and senior stakeholders. Interest in applied AI beyond the theory.
