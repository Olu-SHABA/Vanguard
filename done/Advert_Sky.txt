What You'll Do

Join a team that's shaping the future of Sky through data. Using machine learning, AI and advanced analytics, our Data Scientists solve high-impact customer and commercial challenges at scale. From personalised experiences and intelligent decisioning to next-generation AI solutions, we work with cutting-edge technology and vast data assets to deliver measurable business value. If you're passionate about turning data into action and making a real-world impact, you'll thrive in our team.

As Part Of Our Team, You Will

    Be responsible for developing and driving actionable customer intelligence from our core data assets using advanced analytics
    Develop advanced analytics algorithms exploiting our rich data assets including product holding, demographics, product usage, customer service interactions and customer metrics
    Develop predictive, forecasting and optimisation models using large-scale customer and commercial data, supporting use cases such as churn prediction, customer lifetime value, segmentation, demand forecasting, elasticity modelling and intelligent decisioning
    Collaborate with senior data scientists and stakeholders to define project objectives, develop analytical frameworks, and translate business requirements into analytical solutions
    Engage with our technology teams and data engineers to build compliant, efficient and scalable solutions for managing customer treatments at Sky
    Strengthen our internal data science capabilities by continuously innovating and driving new ideas, engaging in training

What You'll Bring

    Proven experience in at least two advanced analytical areas including, but not limited to Predictive Modelling, Optimisation, Unsupervised Learning and NLP in a commercial setting
    Experience building and deploying large-scale data science solutions (preferably within B2C companies) in a cloud environment (we use GCP VertexAI in the team)
    Very good Python programming skills and experience with Machine Learning libraries such as scikit-learn, XGBoost, Keras, Tensorflow etc
    Familiarity with ETL concepts and ability to write scalable SQL code (familiarity with BigQuery and working with nested data a plus)
    Ability to present your results concisely to both technical and non-technical audiences
    Ability to quickly understand a business objective, problem-solving to deliver a data science solution delivering commercial value
