Lead Data Engineer

Salary Range: £92,701 - £109,060

Location: Edinburgh, Leeds, Manchester, Bristol

Hours: Full time (35 hours)

Working Pattern: Hybrid Working, Job Share
Job Description Summary

At Lloyds Banking Group, we are transforming how we protect our customers, colleagues and services in an increasingly sophisticated threat landscape. The Security Data and AI lab within the Chief Security Office is at the forefront of this transformation - engineering data platforms and AI-driven capabilities that enable intelligent, proactive defence.

We're looking for a Lead Data Engineer to shape and lead the lab’s data engineering capability. This is a senior role combining deep technical expertise with leadership, influencing direction and delivering high-impact outcomes across a complex enterprise environment. You’ll operate at the intersection of data engineering, security and AI, defining and driving the data engineering strategy and roadmap, owning scalable data pipelines, embedding secure-by-design principles, and enabling production-grade data foundations for AI, machine learning and advanced analytics. You’ll also partner across Security, Cloud, Architecture and Product teams while mentoring engineers and building a high-performing engineering culture.
What we're looking for?

    Proven experience leading technical delivery and mentoring engineers in a senior or lead capacity
    Strong experience designing, building and operating scalable data platforms and pipelines in complex environments
    Experience with cloud-native data platforms across Azure, AWS, GCP or similar enterprise environments
    Deep understanding of data modelling, data quality, metadata, lineage and governance principles
    Ability to make pragmatic, risk-aware technical decisions balancing resilience, pace and long-term maintainability
    Experience embedding engineering best practices such as CI/CD, automated testing, observability and version control
    Strong collaboration skills, working across engineering, security, data science and product teams

And it would be great if you had any of the following...

    Exposure to MLOps or SMLOps tooling and model lifecycle management
    Experience supporting AI/ML pipelines, feature engineering or analytics platforms
    Knowledge of AI governance and secure adoption of generative or agentic AI
    Experience working in security-focused or regulated environments
    Familiarity with event-driven or streaming data architectures
